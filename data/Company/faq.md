# Company FAQ

## General Questions

### What are the company's core values?
Our core values are: Innovation, Integrity, Collaboration, Customer Focus, and Excellence. These values guide everything we do from product development to customer service.

### When was the company founded?
The company was founded in 2010 and has grown from a small startup to a mid-sized enterprise with over 500 employees across 3 offices.

### What is the company's mission?
Our mission is to empower businesses with innovative technology solutions that drive growth, efficiency, and competitive advantage.

## Employment

### How do I apply for a job?
All job openings are posted on our careers page at careers.company.com. Submit your application through our online portal with your resume and cover letter.

### What is the hiring process?
1. Application review (1-2 weeks)
2. Initial phone screen (30 minutes)
3. Technical assessment (for technical roles)
4. On-site interviews (3-4 hours)
5. Reference checks
6. Offer extended

### What benefits do you offer?
We offer comprehensive benefits including:
- Health, dental, and vision insurance
- 401(k) with company matching
- 20 days annual leave
- 10 days sick leave
- Professional development budget
- Flexible work arrangements
- Remote work options

### What is the dress code?
We have a business casual dress code. Jeans are permitted on Fridays. For client meetings, business formal attire is expected.

## Work Environment

### What are the working hours?
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible hours are available with manager approval.

### Can I work remotely?
Yes, we offer remote work options. Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote positions are available for certain roles.

### What is the policy on overtime?
Overtime is not generally required. If overtime is necessary, it must be approved in advance by your manager. Overtime is compensated either through time off in lieu or overtime pay, depending on your employment agreement.

## Technology

### What equipment will I receive?
Full-time employees receive:
- Laptop (MacBook Pro or Dell XPS)
- Monitor
- Keyboard and mouse
- Phone (for roles requiring client communication)

### Can I use my own device?
For security reasons, company-issued devices are required for all work-related activities. Personal devices can be used for email and calendar access only.

### What software do you use?
Our standard software stack includes:
- Microsoft Office 365
- Slack for communication
- Jira for project management
- GitHub for code repository
- Google Workspace for collaboration

### How do I get access to systems?
Access is provisioned by IT on your first day. You'll receive login credentials and instructions for all required systems.

## HR Policies

### How do I request time off?
Submit leave requests through the HR portal at hr.company.com. Requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and require manager approval.

### What is the policy on sick leave?
Employees receive 10 sick days per year. Notify your manager on the first day of illness. Medical certificates are required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

### How do I report harassment?
Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited. Report incidents to HR at hr@company.com or through the anonymous reporting hotline at 555-0155. All reports are investigated confidentially.

### What is the performance review process?
Performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Quarterly check-ins with your manager provide ongoing feedback. Performance-based bonuses are awarded for exceptional performance.

## IT Support

### How do I get IT help?
Contact IT support at support@company.com or extension 200. For urgent issues, call the 24/7 hotline at 555-0123.

### What do I do if I forget my password?
Use the self-service password reset portal at password.company.com. If you're locked out, contact IT support for assistance.

### How do I set up VPN?
Download the VPN client from the IT portal at it.company.com/vpn. Follow the setup guide in the IT documentation folder.

### What should I do if my laptop has issues?
Try the troubleshooting steps in the IT documentation. If issues persist, submit a support ticket through the IT portal or call extension 200.

## Facilities

### What are the office hours?
Office buildings are accessible 24/7 with your employee badge. Main reception is staffed from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

### Is parking available?
Free parking is available at all office locations. EV charging stations are available at the main office.

### Are meals provided?
Complimentary coffee and snacks are available in break rooms. Catered lunch is provided on Wednesdays. Vending machines are available for other food options.

### Can I bring my pet?
Only service animals are allowed in the office. Pet-friendly events are organized occasionally.

## Learning & Development

### What training opportunities are available?
Each employee has an annual training budget of $2,000. This can be used for courses, conferences, certifications, and books.

### Is there a mentorship program?
Yes, we have a formal mentorship program. New employees are paired with experienced mentors for their first 6 months.

### How do I request training?
Submit training requests through the HR portal. Include the training details, cost, and justification for how it benefits your role.

## Travel

### What is the travel policy?
Travel for business purposes is approved based on project needs. Book travel through the company portal to ensure compliance with travel policies.

### How do I get reimbursed for expenses?
Submit expense reports through the finance portal within 30 days of incurring the expense. Include receipts for all expenses over $25.

### What is the per diem allowance?
Per diem rates vary by location. Current rates are available in the travel policy document on the HR portal.

## Communication

### How should I communicate with colleagues?
Use Slack for quick messages and Microsoft Teams for video calls. Email is appropriate for formal communication and documentation.

### What is the policy on social media?
Be mindful that your social media presence reflects on the company. Do not share confidential information. The social media policy is available on the HR portal.

### How do I stay updated on company news?
Company announcements are sent via email and posted on the intranet. All-hands meetings are held monthly.

## Security

### What are the security policies?
All employees must complete annual security training. Security policies are available on the IT portal. Report security incidents immediately to security@company.com.

### How do I report a lost badge?
Report lost badges immediately to security at extension 300. Temporary badges are available from reception.

### What is the policy on data privacy?
Protect all confidential company and customer data. The data privacy policy is mandatory reading for all employees.
