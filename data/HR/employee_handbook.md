# Employee Handbook

## Welcome to the Company

Welcome to our organization! This handbook provides essential information about your employment, company policies, and workplace expectations.

## Employment Policies

### Working Hours
- Standard work hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday
- Lunch break: 1 hour (unpaid)
- Flexible hours available with manager approval

### Attendance
- Employees are expected to be punctual and regular in attendance
- Notify your supervisor if you will be late or absent
- Excessive absenteeism may lead to disciplinary action

### Leave Policy

#### Annual Leave
- Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per year
- Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month
- Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
- Manager approval is required for all leave requests

#### Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to 10 sick days per year
- Sick leave can be used for personal illness or family illness
- Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days

#### Casual Leave
- 5 casual leave days per year for personal emergencies
- Cannot be combined with annual leave
- Requires same-day notification to manager

#### Maternity/Paternity Leave
- Maternity leave: 12 weeks paid leave
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks paid leave
- Additional unpaid leave available upon request

## Benefits

### Health Insurance
- Comprehensive health insurance coverage for all full-time employees
- Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision
- Employee contribution: 20% of premium
- Dependents can be added at additional cost

### Retirement Plan
- 401(k) plan with company matching
- Company matches 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of salary
- Vesting period: 3 years

### Professional Development
- Annual training budget: $2,000 per employee
- Conference attendance: up to 2 conferences per year
- Tuition reimbursement for job-related courses

## Workplace Conduct

### Code of Conduct
- Treat all colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited
- Maintain confidentiality of company information
- Use company resources responsibly

### Dress Code
- Business casual attire is expected
- Jeans permitted on Fridays
- Client meetings require formal business attire

### Communication
- Use company email for all business communications
- Response time expectation: within 24 hours for internal emails
- Use appropriate language in all communications

## Performance Management

### Performance Reviews
- Annual performance review conducted in December
- Quarterly check-ins with manager
- Performance-based bonuses available

### Promotion Criteria
- Minimum 1 year in current position
- Consistent performance ratings of "exceeds expectations"
- Demonstrated leadership potential
- Availability of position

## Termination

### Resignation
- Minimum notice period: 2 weeks
- Submit resignation in writing to HR and manager
- Exit interview required

### Termination for Cause
- Grounds for immediate termination include:
  - Theft or fraud
  - Violation of confidentiality agreements
  - Repeated policy violations
  - Insubordination
