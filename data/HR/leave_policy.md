# Leave Policy

## Overview

This document outlines the company's comprehensive leave policy for all employees. All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the respective manager.

## Types of Leave

### 1. Annual Leave

**Eligibility**
- All full-time employees who have completed their probationary period
- Part-time employees receive pro-rated leave based on hours worked

**Entitlement**
- 0-2 years of service: 20 days per year
- 2-5 years of service: 25 days per year
- 5+ years of service: 30 days per year

**Accrual**
- Leave accrues monthly on the last day of each month
- No leave can be taken in advance of accrual
- Maximum carryover: 5 days to the next year

**Application Process**
- Submit request at least 14 days in advance
- Manager approval required within 7 days
- HR notification automatic upon approval

### 2. Sick Leave

**Entitlement**
- 10 days per calendar year
- Additional 5 days for chronic conditions with medical documentation

**Usage**
- Can be used for personal illness or immediate family illness
- Same-day notification required for unplanned sick leave
- Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days

**Documentation**
- Medical certificates must be submitted within 5 days of return
- Certificates must be from licensed medical practitioners
- HR reserves the right to verify authenticity

### 3. Casual Leave

**Entitlement**
- 5 days per calendar year
- Non-cumulative (expires at year-end)

**Purpose**
- Personal emergencies
- Family emergencies
- Short-notice personal matters

**Restrictions**
- Cannot be combined with annual leave
- Cannot be used immediately before or after public holidays
- Requires same-day notification to manager

### 4. Maternity Leave

**Eligibility**
- Female employees with minimum 6 months of service
- Must provide medical certificate confirming pregnancy

**Entitlement**
- 12 weeks of paid leave
- Can start up to 4 weeks before expected due date
- 6 weeks mandatory post-delivery

**Benefits**
- Full salary during leave period
- Health insurance coverage continues
- Job position guaranteed for 6 months post-leave

### 5. Paternity Leave

**Eligibility**
- Male employees with minimum 6 months of service
- Must provide proof of birth/adoption

**Entitlement**
- 2 weeks of paid leave
- Must be taken within 3 months of birth/adoption
- Can be split into two 1-week periods

### 6. Bereavement Leave

**Entitlement**
- Immediate family: 5 days paid leave
- Extended family: 3 days paid leave

**Definition**
- Immediate family: spouse, children, parents, siblings
- Extended family: grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles

### 7. Public Holidays

**Entitlement**
- All employees entitled to all nationally recognized public holidays
- If holiday falls on weekend, compensatory leave provided

**List of Public Holidays**
- New Year's Day: January 1
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Third Monday in January
- Presidents' Day: Third Monday in February
- Memorial Day: Last Monday in May
- Independence Day: July 4
- Labor Day: First Monday in September
- Columbus Day: Second Monday in October
- Veterans Day: November 11
- Thanksgiving Day: Fourth Thursday in November
- Christmas Day: December 25

## Leave Application Process

### Online Portal
1. Log into HR portal
2. Navigate to Leave Management
3. Select leave type and dates
4. Add reason (required for sick/casual leave)
5. Submit for manager approval

### Approval Workflow
- Manager receives notification immediately
- Manager must approve/reject within 7 business days
- Automatic escalation to HR if no response after 7 days
- Employee receives notification of decision

### Emergency Leave
- For emergencies requiring immediate leave
- Contact manager directly (phone/Slack)
- Follow up with online application within 24 hours
- HR notification automatic

## Leave Balances

### Checking Balances
- Available on HR portal dashboard
- Updated in real-time
- Includes accrued, used, and remaining leave

### Year-End Processing
- Unused annual leave: maximum 5 days carried forward
- Unused casual leave: expires December 31
- Sick leave: resets to 10 days on January 1

## Policy Violations

### Unapproved Absence
- First offense: Written warning
- Second offense: Suspension without pay
- Third offense: Termination

### Leave Fraud
- Providing false medical certificates
- Taking unapproved leave
- Immediate termination for cause

## Special Circumstances

### Jury Duty
- Paid leave for jury duty service
- Must provide summons documentation
- Position held during service period

### Military Service
- Unpaid leave for military training
- Reinstatement guaranteed upon return
- Benefits continue during service

### Study Leave
- Up to 5 days per year for exams
- Must provide exam schedule
- Manager approval required
